Where first-time buyers are looking

Every part of Dallas comes with a different mix of price, commute, and character. Here's where many of our buyers start.

  • Oak Cliff Across the Trinity from downtown

    Older brick homes on tree-lined streets, close to the shops and restaurants of the Bishop Arts District.

  • Lake Highlands Northeast Dallas

    Mid-century ranch houses near White Rock Lake and its trails.

  • Garland East of Dallas

    One of the more affordable nearby suburbs, with light rail into downtown.

  • Richardson North of Dallas

    Home to UT Dallas and a large tech job corridor, with established neighborhoods and parks.

  • Mesquite East of Dallas

    Bigger lots for the money and a small-town feel. Yes, the one with the rodeo.

  • Irving West of Dallas

    Next to DFW Airport and the Las Colinas business district, a short commute for many jobs.
